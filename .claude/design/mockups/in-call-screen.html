In-call screen

The call, the self and partner tiles, the sticker tray and the drawing pad, quiet over video. Draft for Adam, 20 October 2026, built from decisions C1–C18 and Adam's answers N1–N7.

Every phone is an iPhone 11 at 414 × 896 pt. Video is illustrated placeholder. Dashed outlines and dark labels inside a phone are annotations, not UI.

Notes for review

Design calls the decisions didn't cover

  • Name and timer in a frosted pill at the top centre, so no snap corner covers them.
  • Tray ✕ on the left of the tab row, matching the pad's ✕, so Draw ↔ tray keeps the close button still.
  • Stickers button on the far left of the bar when you are big, away from End call.
  • Control bar stays above the 40% dim in the medium-tile states.
  • Small tile shadow: a soft shadow and no outline, the one floating thing over video.
  • End call fill is dark-mode Danger (#FF7A6E) with dark ink icon, so it follows C2 like everything else. Contrast is about 6.9:1.
  • Trash is frosted with a Danger icon, not a red fill, so it can't be mistaken for End call.
  • Panel cells have no outlines, only the Background tone step. Outlines are kept for the ✕, selected tab, field, Add and canvas.
  • Hint line "Drag one onto your face" under the Stickers grid.
  • Tab order follows Adam's list: Stickers, Emoji, Text, Draw, Hats, Glasses, Filters. The row scrolls and the selected pill is always scrolled fully into view.
  • Emoji strip: 72 pt cells with no boxes, a 13 pt label above the first column of each category, and 20 pt between categories.
  • Category chips sit at the bottom of the panel, nine 44 pt slots edge to edge with 36 pt discs. The current one gets a Divider-tone disc, not Accent.
  • Filters apply on tap and keep the tray open. Swatches preview your own face, and None comes first.
  • Your camera off: your small tile shows your initial on NotePaperBlue, like Sam's camera-off view.

Still open

  • Hats and glasses: drag onto the face like a sticker, or tap to wear and snap to the head? Probably tap, one of each at a time. Can wait for the hats ticket.
  • Do hats and glasses count toward the 10-sticker cap?
  • The full emoji set is large (about 1,900 with skin tones folded in). Should long-press offer skin tones, or show only the default yellow?

Where the skills were overruled

  • taste-skill bans glass and shadows. C1's frosted controls and the floating tile's shadow are kept because the brief chose them and video needs them to stay legible.
  • ui-ux-pro-max prefers springs and scale press feedback. Motion here is fades and ease-outs only (C18); a press darkens the fill over 0.1 s.
  • Type is SF Pro Rounded (Nunito stands in on the web), not either skill's font picks.
