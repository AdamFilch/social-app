Sounds

A shared soundboard for two. Either of you records a short clip, names it, and it's on both phones. In a call, tapping one plays it for both of you; outside a call it plays just for you. The tab is a grid of tiles you tap without thinking, with "+ Record" always first.

Every phone is an iPhone 11 at 414 × 896 pt, seen as Adam unless it says Sam. Each phone is labelled light or dark; the switch shows them all in one mode. Call chrome is always dark; only the tray panel follows the mode.

S1–S25 are settled with Adam. M1–M18 are mine, made to fill the gaps. A1–A5 are Adam's answers to the v1 questions.

Sounds tab

Opened on the sofa, or mid-chat to play something back. It's a launcher, so tiles are big, all the same size, and tapping one does one thing.

1 · Empty, first run Light

S16M2M16
  • The grid still starts with "+ Record" in its usual first slot, so the empty tab looks like the full one with no tiles yet. There's no big empty-state button that would vanish later.
  • Copy under the grid: "No sounds yet. Record one for Sam?" then "Up to 10 seconds. Sounds you save show up for you both." That second line covers S6 and the shared library in one go.
  • The copy goes once there's one sound, from either of you (S23).
  • First tap on "+ Record" triggers the system mic prompt. If it's denied, the dialog says "buttercup can't use the microphone. Turn it on in Settings" with an "Open Settings" button.

2 · Full grid, one playing Dark

S3S14S22M1M3
  • "Miso's meow" is about 60% through. The tint fills at the clip's own pace (linear, because it's real progress) and clears as soon as it ends.
  • The stop badge tells you a second tap stops it (S14). Tapping "Ba dum tss" stops the meow and starts that one: one tile clears, the other starts filling.
  • Sounds without an emoji show 🔊 ("Good morning song").
  • Long titles get two lines and then an ellipsis ("Sam's dinosaur noise, the long one"). The full title is in VoiceOver and in Edit.
  • It plays even with the silent switch on (A2), because it only plays when you tap it.
  • Fourteen sounds run past the fold. The grid scrolls vertically under the title, and the tab bar stays put.
  • The tint is a separate PlayTint colour: Accent mixed into Surface (45% light, 30% dark), so the title keeps its contrast (see the proposals at the end).

3 · Long-press menu Light

S10S7M7
  • Long-press "Ribbit": the system lifts the tile and blurs the rest, with Edit and Delete underneath. Delete is in Danger red with a trash icon.
  • It's the native .contextMenu, so it behaves like everywhere else on iOS. Tap outside to close.
  • "+ Record" has no menu and can't be moved.
  • The lift is iOS's own, which includes a small scale. That bends the "no scale" rule; see flag F2.

4 · Reorder, dragging Dark

S17S18S19M7
  • From state 3, keep holding "Ribbit" and move: it's heading for the spot after "Miso's meow". The menu goes and the tile follows your finger 1:1.
  • A dashed slot shows where it will land. Other tiles shift into their new places with a 0.2 s ease-out. They don't bounce.
  • Let go and the tile drops into the slot. The new order is saved for both of you, and Sam's grid changes live.
  • The slot before "+ Record" isn't a drop target. Dragging there keeps the tile in the first sound slot.
  • Dragging near the top or bottom edge scrolls the grid.
  • VoiceOver and Switch Control get "Move earlier" and "Move later" actions on each tile, so dragging isn't the only way.

Record and name

Outside a call. Both steps happen in one dialog card over the dimmed tab, so you can still see where the sound will land.

5 · Recording Light

S6S8M4M5
  • One tap on "+ Record" and you're recording (M4). The card opens with the usual 0.2 s fade. A noise at the start can be trimmed off later.
  • Header: a static red dot and "Recording", in words, so colour isn't the only signal. ✕ stops the recording and asks "Discard this sound?" (state 6c). Keep goes on to naming it.
  • The track is the whole 10 s. Level bars build left to right as you talk, so one shape shows both "it's hearing me" and "how long is left".
  • "0:04" on the left and "6 s left" on the right, in tabular digits so nothing jitters.
  • The 88 pt Accent Stop sits under the thumb. At 10 s it stops on its own and moves to step 6, the same as tapping Stop.
  • Tapping the scrim does nothing while recording or naming, so a stray tap can't lose the clip.
  • If the app goes to the background, the recording is discarded, the same rule as S21.

6 · Name this sound, empty Dark

S9M6
  • The recording content crossfades into the naming step (0.15 s). The card moves up to sit 12 pt above the keyboard, at the keyboard's own speed and curve.
  • The field has focus and a visible "Name" label. The placeholder ("Like Miso's meow") is only a hint. 30 characters at most, with the count shown.
  • Save is disabled (40%, no sticker) until there's a non-space character. Return also saves once it's enabled.
  • Play plays the whole clip on your phone. The play button turns into stop, and a red playhead runs along the track.
  • Discard is a plain text button. It asks "Discard this sound?" first (state 6c, A3).

6b · Name this sound, ready Light

S9S20
  • Typed "Miso's meow" and tapped Play. The playhead is about halfway, and the button now reads as stop.
  • Save comes to full strength with its sticker. Under the scrim, "+ Record" has dropped its shadow (M2), so Save is the only sticker on screen.
  • Save closes the card with a 0.15 s fade. The new tile fades in right after "+ Record", and the others move along one place with a 0.2 s ease-out (S20).
  • A failed save keeps the card open with "Couldn't save. Try again" under the field, in Danger, and an .error haptic (T3).

6c · Discard this sound? Dark

A3S9
  • From Discard in the naming card, or ✕ while recording. The keyboard goes down, and the confirm opens over the naming card, which is dimmed by a second scrim.
  • Same wording pattern as "Discard this note?": the title asks, and the body says what happens: "You'll lose this recording. Sam never hears it."
  • "Keep" goes back to the naming card with the keyboard up and the name kept. "Discard" (Danger, trash icon) closes both cards with a 0.15 s fade.
  • A tap on the scrim counts as Keep. There's no haptic.
  • In a call, the same confirm opens over the call screen from the tray's ✕. If the call ends, the clip still goes without asking (S21).

Edit and delete

For later fixes, reached from the long-press menu. Trimming and emoji live only here, so saving a new sound stays two steps.

7 · Edit sheet Dark

S10S11S12M8M9
  • A system sheet at the large detent with the house header: Cancel, "Edit sound", Save (the sheet's confirming action, with the sticker).
  • Name: the same field as when you saved it, prefilled. Save is disabled if it's empty.
  • Emoji: a 64 pt well. Tapping it opens the emoji keyboard; the first emoji typed replaces the old one and closes the keyboard. "Remove" goes back to 🔊.
  • Trim: drag the Accent handles in from either end. The parts you cut are dimmed, and the kept part is framed in Outline. Times sit under the handles, and the kept length shows next to the label.
  • "Play trimmed" plays only the kept part, with the red playhead. The handles have 44 pt hit areas, and dragging a handle shows its time.
  • Trimming saves new start and end points; the original audio is kept (S11).
  • If you change anything and then Cancel, it asks "Discard changes?", the same as the drawn-note sheet.

8 · Delete confirm Light

S7M10
  • The house dialog card. Title: "Delete “Ribbit”?" Body: "It goes for both of you. Sam won't have it either." That says exactly what happens.
  • "Keep it" and "Delete" are equal width, with Delete on the right, in Danger with a trash icon. There's no sticker on either.
  • A tap on the scrim keeps it, so there's no ✕ (a small departure from the dialog card; flag F4).
  • The tile fades out over 0.15 s and the rest move up with a 0.2 s ease-out. If Sam is playing it at that moment, it stops on his phone.

In a call

The same library, from the effects tray. You're mid-conversation, so the tray stays open and each tap is one sound for both of you.

S1S2S24S25M11

Motion

  • Play: the tile tint fills linearly over the clip's length, then fades out over 0.15 s. Stopping fades it out at once.
  • Record dialog: it opens like the Notes dialog (0.2 s fade, 0.96 to 1 scale, ease-out) and closes with a 0.15 s fade. Recording to naming is a 0.15 s crossfade inside the card.
  • Level bars: each new bar appears at full height, with no grow-in. They're live input, not decoration, and they stop when recording stops.
  • Reorder: the dragged tile follows the finger 1:1. The others move 0.2 s ease-out. The drop is the same ease, with no overshoot.
  • New or deleted tile: a 0.15 s opacity fade, and the neighbours move 0.2 s ease-out.
  • Partner pill: fades in 0.2 s, out 0.15 s.
  • Reduce Motion: neighbour moves and the dialog scale become crossfades. The play tint becomes a static half-tint plus the stop badge, so the state still shows without movement.
  • Nothing idles. The empty tab and the partner pill are still.

Haptics

  • None of our own on play, stop, record, save, discard, reorder or delete (M15). These are ordinary taps, and T3 keeps them quiet.
  • The system context menu gives its usual long-press tap. It's built in, and we don't add another.
  • .error when the mic won't start or a save fails (T3).
  • The tab bar's .selection stays. None on the tray's Sounds pill, as with every tray tab.

Flags against design.md

Places where the settled decisions, or what iOS gives us, rub against the system.

  • F1 Tray access, resolved (A1). The Stickers button is always in the bar and opens on Sounds when stickers aren't possible. This amends the in-call spec; see the next section.
  • F2 System lift, accepted. The native context menu and drag preview keep iOS's own small scale and spring.
  • F3 One sticker per screen, resolved. "+ Record" drops its shadow while a dialog or sheet covers the tab, so only the card's Save carries one.
  • F4 Dialog card closing. design.md says ✕ or a scrim tap closes it. Recording and naming ignore the scrim (unsaved work), and the delete confirm uses "Keep it" instead of ✕.
  • F5 Tab bar. "Three equal tabs" in §6 becomes four.

Amendment to the in-call spec

For the spec run. in-call-screen.md isn't edited here.

  • R4 / C15: the Stickers button is in the control bar in every call state, always first: Stickers · Mute · Camera · Route · End. It used to join only while your own view was big.
  • §3.3 step 1 and N7: the tray opens on the last tab used, except when stickers aren't possible (audio call, camera off, back camera) or Sam is big. Then it opens on Sounds.
  • §8 camera off / back camera: the Stickers button and the tray are no longer disabled. Only the face tabs are dimmed, with the line "Stickers come back when your camera's on". "Add stickers" on the medium tile keeps its disabled state.
  • Partner big: tapping a face pill makes your view big first (state C), then shows that tab.
  • R5 tabs: Stickers · Emoji · Text · Draw · Hats · Glasses · Sounds.

Proposed design-system changes

For the spec run. Not applied.

  • §6 Tab bar: four equal tabs; add the speaker.wave.2 icon.
  • New token PlayTint: light #FFE490 (Accent 45% into Surface), dark #68571F (30%). TextPrimary on it is about 12:1 in light and 6.3:1 in dark. It's used only for "playing now" progress.
  • New component, Sound tile: tab size (116 × 108, Outline) and tray size (120 × 84, Background, no Outline); rest, playing, and the "+ Record" variant.
  • New component, Waveform track: a recording timeline, a playback playhead, and a trim frame with handles.
  • Dialog card: add "may ignore scrim taps while it holds unsaved work".
  • Sticker shadow: add "a creating tile under a scrim drops its shadow, so the card's confirming action is the only sticker".
