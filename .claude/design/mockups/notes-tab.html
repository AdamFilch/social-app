Notes tab

The Notes tab is where Adam and Sam look back at what they've sent each other. It opens on this week with today picked, so the first thing you see is today's notes, big. The month view is one tap away for "what did we send last week?" and "how much have we been writing?".

Every phone is an iPhone 11 at 414 × 896 pt, viewed as Adam on Thursday 29 October 2026, 9:41. The notes are illustrated placeholders. Each phone is labelled light or dark; the switch shows them all in one mode.

D1–D18 were settled by Adam before v1. N1–N10 are mine, made to fill gaps. N2 and N9 follow Adam's answers to v1, and N7 and N10 follow his v2 feedback.

Weekly view

The default on open (D11). The strip pages a week at a time, and the content follows your finger while you swipe. That isn't decorative motion: it's the gesture itself, and it snaps to the week with the system paging curve.

1 · Today, with notes Light

D1D11D13D14N1N2
  • What you see on open: this week (Mon 26 – Sun 1), Thursday 29 picked. The cell is Accent because it's today and has the ring because it's selected.
  • The pill reads the selected day's month, "October". Sunday 1 November sits in this week, but the pill only changes if you pick it.
  • Three tiles, newest first: Sam 9:20, Adam 8:05, Sam 7:12. No tile carries a doodle (N10).
  • This is the current week, so the strip won't page left to next week. Friday to Sunday can still be picked (state 3).
  • Tiles show the note and the sender's initial, nothing else. The time is in the full-size view and in each tile's VoiceOver label ("Note from Sam, 7:12 am").
  • Tapping a strip cell moves the ring with a 0.15 s fade and swaps the grid with the same fade. No haptic.

2 · Past day, after a week swipe Dark

  • No cell is Accent because today isn't in this week. The ring alone shows the selection.

3 · Empty or future day Light

D16
  • Friday 30 is tomorrow. You can pick it, and it says "No notes yet" in TextSecondary, centred, with nothing else.
  • A past empty day (Wednesday 28) looks the same. "Yet" still works: notes arrive late from the offline outbox.
  • No "Draw Sam something?" prompt. "Make a note" is just above and always sends to today, so a prompt on a future day would be misleading.
  • The header reads "Friday 30 · No notes" so the count slot never goes blank.

Month view

For looking back across weeks and seeing who has been writing. It answers "when?" and "who?" at a glance, and you tap a day to read it.

4 · October, current month Light

D6D7D8D9D13N4N8N9
  • Mon–Sun columns. Five rows run from Mon 28 Sep to Sun 1 Nov. Spill days are TextSecondary at 50%, with their dots also at 50%.
  • Each day has two fixed dot places: yours on the left (blue), Sam's on the right (red). A day with only Sam's notes shows a single red dot on the right, so you can tell who wrote from position as well as colour.
  • Dots show only whether someone sent a note that day, not how many. The counts are in the summary.
  • The summary line is "23 notes this month" with a legend under it: "11 from you · 12 from Sam". It works as the dot key and as the D6 summary.
  • Tapping any day, including a spill day, opens its week with that day selected (D18, D9). The crossfade is the same as the toggle's.
  • The month name sits inside the card ("October 2026"). The pill now reads "Current week", which takes you back to today.
  • Edges: October is the current month, so a left swipe does nothing: the grid stays where it is, with no bounce. Swiping right goes back month by month to the month you paired (say August 2026), then stops the same way.
  • Spill days outside the paired range (before you paired) are still shown greyed, but tapping them does nothing. Inside the range they open their week.

5 · October, current month Dark

N5
  • Dark dots are lighter, #79AEFF and #FF7A6E, so they reach 6:1 or more on the dark Surface.
  • On the Accent today cell, the dot rings switch to OnAccent. The cream Outline would almost disappear on yellow.
  • Swiping the grid changes month. The summary updates with it, and its count uses tabular digits so it doesn't jump.
  • VoiceOver reads each day as "Tuesday 27 October, notes from Sam". The grid offers "Next month" and "Previous month" as actions, so the swipe isn't the only way to move.

Note dialog

A dialog over the Notes tab, opening on the tapped note. You can still see the tab behind it, so you stay oriented. To close it, tap ✕ or anywhere on the dimmed area.

6 · Viewing note 1 of 3 Light

D4D5N2N7
  • Opened from the first tile in state 1, Sam's 9:20 note, the newest. The card's header has the sender's avatar, "Sam", "Thursday 29 Oct · 9:20 am" and ✕ on the right. That's everything D4 asks for.
  • The card is 374 pt wide with 16 pt padding, so the note is 338 pt square: about twice the tile, with radius 20 and a 2.5 pt Outline. The card is about 450 pt tall and centred.
  • Behind it, the scrim is black at 42% in light mode and 60% in dark mode. The card's Outline keeps it separate from the dark background.
  • Opening: the scrim fades in, and the card fades and scales from 0.96 to 1, both over 0.2 s ease-out. Closing is a 0.15 s fade. With Reduce Motion it's a fade only. There's no haptic and no zoom from the tile.
  • The viewer pages in the grid's order. Swiping left goes to the next page, which is older: 9:20 → 8:05 → 7:12. The pager shows where you are; VoiceOver reads "Note 1 of 3". The sender and time update with each page.
  • No wrap-around: the first and last notes are where the swipe stops. You can't swipe into another day.
  • Closing: ✕ (44 pt) or a tap on the scrim. Swipe down isn't used, because the note area's own horizontal swipe is the only gesture inside the card. Both ways of closing go back to the same grid, unchanged.
  • Room for the later actions: Favourite, Save and Unsend will go in one row of small 44 pt pills under the pager. That adds about 58 pt, making the card about 508 pt, which still centres with plenty of margin on the smallest supported phone. Nothing is drawn for them yet.

The toggle button

It has to look tappable (D10), so it uses the Small secondary button: a 40 pt Surface pill with a 2 pt Outline and a 44 pt hit area. It gets no Accent fill, because "Make a note" is the screen's one primary action.

Behaviour

  • Week view, "October": opens the month of the selected day. If you've swiped to a week in September, it reads "September".
  • Month view, "Current week": always goes to today's week with today selected, whatever month you've swiped to. It's how you get home.
  • Long labels: "September" is the widest month name. The pill grows to the left and the title stays put. At accessibility text sizes the title row wraps, and the pill moves under the title, left-aligned.
  • VoiceOver: "Show month, October, button" and "Show current week, button".
  • Pressed: the fill darkens 6% toward TextPrimary over 0.1 s. No scale, no haptic.

Motion

Week0.18 s ease-out crossfadeMonth
  • Everything below "Make a note" crossfades. The title, toggle and button stay still; only the toggle's label fades between words.
  • Selecting a day: the ring and the grid fade over 0.15 s.
  • Swipes follow the finger and page. No overshoot. At the paired or current edge the page doesn't move.
  • With Reduce Motion: the crossfade stays, and the dialog's scale is dropped, leaving just the fade.
  • Haptics: none anywhere on this screen. The tab bar's own .selection haptic is unchanged.

Design-system changes for the spec run

  • design.md §7 (Notes) and §10 (Doodles): the Notes tab has no doodle. Remove "the doodle is the tape on the newest tile" from §7. In §10, doodle-tape has no place left; either drop it or keep it unused for later.
  • design.md §6 Note tile: remove "the tape strip sits on the newest tile in the Notes grid only".
  • New component, Dialog card: a centred Surface card with radius 28 (radius.sheet), 2.5 pt Outline, 16 pt padding and a 44 pt ✕ icon button. The scrim is black at 42% (light) or 60% (dark). It opens with a 0.2 s fade and 0.96 → 1 scale, ease-out, and closes with a 0.15 s fade. ✕ or a tap on the scrim closes it. No sticker.
  • Week strip: add "ring = selected, Accent = today" (N1).
  • Tokens: NoteDotMine and NoteDotPartner, below.

Proposed tokens

The dots carry meaning, so they need 3:1 against the card. NotePaperBlue is too pale for that (1.4:1 on Surface), so I'm proposing two new colour sets. The light red is the existing NoteInkRed, and the dark red is the dark Danger value. They're named for their role so Danger keeps meaning "something went wrong".

TokenLightDarkOn Surface L / DOn Accent L / DRole
NoteDotMine new#2F6FDB#79AEFF4.67 / 6.833.02 / 1.49*Your notes dot, your count in the legend
NoteDotPartner new#E5483B#FF7A6E3.87 / 6.062.51 / 1.67*Partner's notes dot, their count in the legend

* On the today cell, each dot's 1.5 pt OnAccent ring (11.6:1 against Accent) marks the shape, so the fill doesn't have to. Blue and red stay distinct for the common kinds of colour blindness, and position (left = you) backs them up.
