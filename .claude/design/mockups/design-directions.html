Buttercup Field by day, Sticky Note by night

Buttercup yellow is the action and leaf green means "new to you", in both modes. Light mode sits on a warm cream with dark leaf-green ink. Dark mode uses Sticky Note's brown-black paper, with the cream used as ink. Every outline, ring and sticker comes from the shared foundations. Mode follows the phone's setting.

How the two modes stay one brand

  • Same roles. Accent is always buttercup yellow and is always the fill for the thing you can do. Highlight is always leaf green and only means "new to you".
  • Shared cream. Light mode's Background (#FFF8E2) is dark mode's TextPrimary, so the cream that is paper by day becomes ink by night.
  • Tuned hexes. Dark Accent moves from Sticky Note's lemon #FFD84A to buttercup #FFCB2E. Dark Highlight moves from Sticky Note's pink to leaf green #8BD06E. Dark Outline warms to #EDE3CC so it matches the cream text.
  • Accent is fill only. In light mode it is 1.5:1 on cream, so it never carries text or meaning by itself. It always sits inside an outline, with OnAccent text (9.5:1 and 11.7:1).
  • Divider is a quiet 1.2:1 by design. Rows are also separated by 62 pt of height, so the line is only a hint.
  • NotePaper pastels don't change with the mode, because they are part of the note image. Ink on each is 10:1 or better.

Calling

The partner card puts the two call buttons in thumb reach. They are equal buttercup pills at 56 pt and neither gets a sticker shadow. Calling has no single primary action, so this tab has no sticker. The latest note's green ring and "New" badge are the one highlight. The flower next to the title is the tab's doodle.

Notes

"Make a note" is the screen's one sticker-shadowed action. The week strip previews the calendar: each day stacks its notes as small cards, and today is a buttercup cell. Today's notes sit in a two-column grid of widget-shaped tiles, each tagged with the sender's initial. The unseen one has the ring. The tape on the newest tile is the doodle.

Hobbies

One card per hobby. Both people's initials sit in the header, and a check badge plus a sentence ("You read today · Sam hasn't yet") shows who did it today, so colour isn't the only signal. Your current item has a progress bar and a 48 pt +1 button. The partner's item sits underneath in secondary text. Every +1 is equal, so there's no sticker. The sparkle by the title is the doodle.

Changes from the draft

  • One direction: Buttercup Field light with Sticky Note dark, tuned to share roles (see Tokens).
  • Call buttons are equal. Both are buttercup pills, 56 pt, with no sticker. The sticker shadow moves to the one creating action on screens that have one ("Make a note"). Calling and Hobbies have none.
  • All buttons are pills. The draft's 16 pt button corners looked half-decided next to the round tab pill and avatars. One shape for every tappable control reads as more deliberate.
  • "New" is now in sentence case. The badge changed from "NEW" to "New" because tiny, spaced-out capitals are harder to read and feel generic.
  • Avatars are initials on a stable NotePaper tint (Adam blue, Sam pink). The same tint appears wherever that person shows up, and a photo can replace the letter later inside the same outlined circle.
  • New tokens OnAccent (replaces the "ink on accent" rule) and the five NotePaper pastels. The draft's per-direction tables are gone.
  • Pressed state stays as the draft proposed: the sticker shadow drops to 0 and the fill mixes 12% OnAccent, over 0.1 s, with no scale.
